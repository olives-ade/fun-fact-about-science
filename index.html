Why do leaves change color?

When daylight fades, chlorophyll breaks down and reveals pigments that were there all along.

SCIENCEDISCOVERY WORLD

Mini experiment

Extract a leaf’s pigment

  1. Crush a green leaf in a clear glass jar.
  2. Add rubbing alcohol to cover the leaf pieces.
  3. Place the jar in warm water for 30 minutes.
  4. Observe the liquid changing to green.

Safety: Ask an adult to help. Do not drink or heat the alcohol directly.
